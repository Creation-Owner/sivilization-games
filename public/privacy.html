1. About Sivilization

The service is operated by Khusan, an individual operating under the brand name Sivilization in Uzbekistan.

In this Privacy Policy, “Sivilization,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to this operator.

Privacy and support contact: sivilization.support@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

The information we process depends on how you interact with Sivilization.

2.1 Account and profile information

When you create or use an account, we may process:

  • Your email address.
  • Your name or profile display name, if provided.
  • Your account identifier.
  • Authentication and session information.
  • Account creation and update information.
  • Your account role, permissions, and membership status.

We use Supabase to support account authentication. Please do not send your password to our support address or include it in public comments.

2.2 Purchase and subscription information

When you purchase content or subscribe, we may process:

  • The films, rentals, games, software, or products associated with your account.
  • Transaction identifiers and purchase dates.
  • Payment amounts, currencies, and payment status.
  • Subscription status and applicable access periods.
  • Cancellation, refund, and dispute-related information.
  • Purchase history and content-access permissions.
  • Rental expiry information, where applicable.

2.3 Wallet-credit information

If your account receives wallet credit, we may process:

  • Your current credit balance.
  • Wallet-purchase amounts and transaction identifiers.
  • Items purchased using credit.
  • Records associated with issuing, adjusting, spending, or restoring credit, where those records are maintained.

Sivilization wallet credit is currently added by the owner. Customers cannot purchase or top up wallet credit with real money.

2.4 Service activity and user-provided content

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Film watch progress and completion status.
  • Items saved to your library, favourites, or cart.
  • Comments you submit.
  • Information you provide when requesting support.
  • Attachments and other material you choose to send us.

Your comments may be visible to other users together with your profile name and, where applicable, an account-role label.

Do not publish sensitive information, payment details, passwords, or another person’s private information in comments.

2.5 Technical information

Our website and service providers may process technical information necessary to deliver, maintain, and secure the service, including:

  • IP addresses.
  • Browser, operating-system, and device information.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Session identifiers.
  • Error, diagnostic, and security logs.
  • Information about requests made to our website or services.

The exact information processed depends on the provider, its configuration, and the features enabled.

2.6 Support communications

When you contact us, we receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address, account details, purchase reference, and description of the issue.

Please provide only information reasonably necessary to resolve your enquiry.

3. How we obtain information

We obtain personal information:

  • Directly from you when you register, purchase, comment, or contact support.
  • Through your use of account and content features.
  • From authentication, infrastructure, and payment providers where necessary to operate the service.
  • From payment notifications and transaction updates.
  • From people reporting an account, content, security, or support issue.

Public website pages can be visited without an account. Account-specific features, purchases, and protected content may require sign-in.

4. Why we use personal information

We use personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage accounts.
  • Provide access to purchased or subscription-based content.
  • Process and reconcile purchases.
  • Maintain wallet balances and wallet-purchase records.
  • Manage rentals, subscriptions, cancellations, and refunds.
  • Save watch progress, favourites, cart contents, and library information.
  • Display and moderate comments.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Send essential account, security, and service communications.
  • Diagnose errors and improve service reliability.
  • Detect and investigate fraud, misuse, and unauthorised access.
  • Enforce applicable service terms.
  • Comply with applicable legal, accounting, and recordkeeping obligations.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not use payment-related customer information for unrelated marketing without an appropriate legal basis and any consent required by applicable law.

5. Grounds for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on the basis appropriate to the processing activity.

Depending on the circumstances and jurisdiction, this may include:

  • Performing an agreement with you, including providing purchased services.
  • Taking steps at your request before entering into an agreement.
  • Complying with legal obligations.
  • Your consent, where required.
  • Legitimate interests in operating, securing, and supporting our service, where that basis is recognised and does not override your rights.
  • Other grounds permitted by applicable law.

Where processing relies on your consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us or using an available consent control. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before consent was withdrawn.

6. Payments and Paddle

Where a purchase is made through Paddle, Paddle acts as the merchant of record for that transaction.

Paddle may process information including your contact details, location, billing information, payment information, and technical information for purposes such as completing transactions, calculating tax, preventing fraud, and meeting legal obligations.

We may receive the customer and transaction information needed to fulfil your purchase, manage access, reconcile payments, and provide support.

Our application is not designed to collect or store full payment-card numbers or card security codes.

Paddle’s processing is governed by its own privacy notice, available at https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases using owner-issued wallet credit are recorded within Sivilization and do not require a new card payment through Paddle.

Sivilization operates in Uzbekistan, but some service providers may process or store information in other countries.

International processing may be subject to legal requirements concerning transfers, safeguards, or local storage. We are responsible for assessing the requirements applicable to our processing and arranging our services accordingly.

12. How long we retain information

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, taking account of applicable legal obligations.

For example:

  • Account information is generally retained while an account remains active.
  • Purchase and payment records may be retained after account closure for accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, or legal requirements.
  • Watch progress, favourites, and cart information may be retained to provide the relevant account features.
  • Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve enquiries and maintain relevant records.
  • Security and technical logs are retained according to operational and security needs.
  • Backup copies may remain until removed or overwritten through the relevant backup process.

We determine retention by considering the type of information, why it is needed, legal requirements, and the risks associated with retaining it.

Closing an account does not necessarily result in immediate deletion of every related record. Information retained for a specific obligation is limited to that purpose.

No website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential. Contact us if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission.

Depending on your location, applicable law, and the circumstances, you may have rights to:

  • Obtain information about how your personal information is processed.
  • Request access to information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request restrictions on processing.
  • Request a portable copy of certain information.
  • Complain to an appropriate authority.

Not every right applies in every situation. Some requests may be limited by legal obligations, another person’s rights, or other lawful exceptions.

Making a request

Email sivilization.support@gmail.com and include:

  • The email address associated with your account.
  • A clear description of your request.
  • Any transaction reference relevant to the request.

We may need to verify your identity before providing information or making changes. We will request only verification information reasonably necessary for that purpose.

Do not send passwords or identity documents unless a specific, necessary verification process has been explained to you.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Content-specific age restrictions may also apply. Relevant restrictions should be explained in the content listing or service terms.
