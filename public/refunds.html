Refund Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

We review requests fairly and consider the product, the circumstances of the purchase, any applicable payment-provider terms, and your legal rights.

Nothing in this policy limits rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to digital purchases and subscriptions offered through Sivilization, including eligible purchases made with owner-issued wallet credit.

It supplements our Terms of Service and the conditions disclosed for the particular product before purchase.

Purchases processed through Paddle are also subject to Paddle’s applicable buyer terms and refund policy. This policy does not override those terms or mandatory consumer rights.

We do not currently sell physical products under this policy.

3. How to request help or a refund

Contact us at sivilization.support@gmail.com.

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your account.
  • The name of the purchased item or subscription.
  • Your transaction identifier or order reference, if available.
  • The purchase date and amount, if known.
  • A description of the issue and the remedy you are requesting.
  • Relevant screenshots or error messages, if useful.

Do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, card security codes, or unnecessary identity documents.

Contact us as soon as reasonably possible after discovering a problem. Any request window in this policy does not shorten a longer period provided by applicable law.

For a Paddle purchase, you may also request assistance directly through Paddle order support.

4. Refund situations we review

Examples of purchase problems and our approach
Situation Our approach
Confirmed duplicate payment We investigate and arrange an appropriate reversal or refund for a confirmed duplicate.
Paid item is not delivered or accessible We investigate the issue, attempt a prompt resolution, and provide an appropriate remedy where required or agreed.
Material defect or significant misdescription We assess the problem and provide the correction, refund, or other remedy required by applicable law or the purchase agreement.
Accidental purchase that has not been used We review requests submitted within fourteen days of purchase. Approval depends on the circumstances and applicable terms.
Content already accessed, downloaded, or used We assess the request individually. Use does not automatically remove rights relating to defects or other mandatory consumer protections.
Purchase using owner-issued wallet credit An approved reversal ordinarily restores the credit used rather than creating a cash payment.

The fourteen-day request window for accidental, unused purchases is a customer-service review policy. It is not an unconditional money-back guarantee and is not a statement that all customers have identical statutory cancellation rights.

Decisions may take account of the product description, access or usage records, the reported issue, and applicable payment-provider requirements.

We may request reasonable information needed to verify a purchase or investigate a problem. We will not use that process to obstruct rights provided by applicable law.

5. Films and rentals

5.1 Film purchases

If a purchased film cannot be accessed, fails to play because of a service-side problem, or differs materially from its description, contact support.

We may first investigate and attempt to restore access or correct the problem, without limiting remedies available under applicable law.

Requests based solely on a change of mind after viewing are assessed individually and are not automatically approved under our voluntary policy.

5.2 Film rentals

Rentals provide access for the period disclosed before purchase. Owner-issued wallet rentals currently provide seven days of access from the successful rental transaction.

The ordinary expiry of a correctly supplied rental does not by itself establish a refund entitlement.

If a technical problem prevents viewing during the rental period, report it promptly. Depending on the circumstances, an appropriate remedy may include restored access, an extension, replacement access, or a refund.

Starting a stream does not automatically remove every refund or withdrawal right. Any applicable exception depends on the law and any required disclosures, consent, and acknowledgement.

6. Games, software, and digital products

Review the product description, delivery method, platform, version, and stated system requirements before purchasing.

If a product is not delivered, has a material defect, or does not meet the description provided before purchase, contact support.

We may request information about your device, software version, and error messages to diagnose the issue.

A compatibility issue involving clearly disclosed requirements may be assessed differently from a defect or inaccurate listing. We will consider the specific circumstances and applicable law.

Downloading or installing a product does not automatically remove rights relating to defects, misdescription, or other mandatory protections.

7. Subscriptions and cancellation

Cancelling a subscription and requesting a refund are separate actions.

7.1 Cancelling renewal

For subscriptions processed through Paddle, use the subscription-management link supplied with your purchase or contact Paddle order support. You may also contact Sivilization for assistance.

Unless different conditions were disclosed or applicable law requires otherwise, cancellation stops future renewal and takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period.

Deleting an application, ceasing to use the service, or requesting account deletion should not be relied upon as a substitute for cancelling recurring billing. Ask support for help if you are unsure whether a cancellation has completed.

7.2 Subscription refund requests

We review subscription refund requests according to the circumstances, including accidental purchases, access failures, material defects, duplicate charges, and applicable legal rights.

We do not promise automatic prorated refunds for unused time after a valid cancellation unless required by law or expressly offered.

If you believe a charge occurred after an effective cancellation, provide the charge details and any cancellation confirmation so the issue can be investigated.

8. Wallet-credit purchases

Sivilization wallet credit is issued by the owner. Customers cannot buy or top up that credit with real money.

If we approve reversal of a purchase paid entirely with owner-issued wallet credit, we ordinarily restore the credit deducted for that purchase.

Such a reversal does not create an entitlement to receive cash for credit that was not purchased with money, subject to applicable law.

If you believe a wallet transaction deducted the wrong amount, was duplicated, or failed while still reducing your balance, contact support. We will investigate the transaction records and correct confirmed errors.

A credit restoration may be accompanied by removal of the entitlement created by the reversed purchase.

9. Purchases processed through Paddle

Where your purchase is processed through Paddle, Paddle acts as the merchant of record.

Its applicable buyer terms, refund policy, transaction rules, and refund decisions apply to those purchases.

You can obtain assistance using your Paddle receipt, order reference, or paddle.net.

  • Paddle Refund Policy
  • Paddle Buyer Terms

We may assist by providing fulfilment information, investigating access problems, or coordinating the request. We cannot guarantee approval of a refund controlled by Paddle.

Nothing in this policy substitutes Sivilization wallet credit for a monetary refund where the applicable transaction terms or law require a monetary refund.

10. Refund method and processing

Approved monetary refunds are processed through the relevant payment provider, ordinarily to the original payment method where supported and required by the transaction rules.

Processing and bank posting times depend on the payment provider, payment method, and financial institution. We do not guarantee a fixed bank posting time.

Approved wallet-purchase reversals are ordinarily handled by restoring the relevant wallet credit.

We will explain any proposed alternative remedy, such as replacement access or an access extension. We will not require you to accept an alternative in place of a refund where you are legally entitled to a refund.

Contact support if an approved remedy has not been completed or if you need an update.

11. Access after a refund

If a purchase is fully refunded or reversed, access granted solely by that purchase may be removed.

We will not intentionally remove unrelated purchases or other valid access entitlements merely because one transaction is refunded.

Where a software licence or download is refunded, you must comply with any applicable requirement to stop using the refunded product and remove unauthorised copies.

Partial refunds or other remedies may have different effects, which should be explained when the remedy is agreed.

12. Mandatory consumer rights

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, or replaces rights that cannot lawfully be waived, including rights concerning defective, misdescribed, or undelivered digital content or services.

Your rights may depend on your country, the product, the transaction, and whether required information and consent were provided.

Any statutory withdrawal exception for immediately supplied digital content applies only where its legal conditions have been met.

Contacting support does not prevent you from using a complaint, payment-dispute, regulatory, or legal process available to you, and does not extend any deadline that applies to those processes.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our products, procedures, payment arrangements, or legal obligations change.

We will publish the revised policy with an updated date and provide additional notice where required.

Changes will not retroactively remove rights associated with completed purchases where doing so would be unlawful.
